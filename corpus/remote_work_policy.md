# Remote Work Policy
**Document ID:** POL-002  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** November 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources & IT Security

---

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corp supports flexible work arrangements. This policy defines the requirements and approval processes for employees who wish to work remotely, whether from home or from a location other than their designated office.

---

## 2. Eligibility

Remote work eligibility depends on the employee's role classification:

| Role Classification | Remote Eligibility |
|---|---|
| Office-Required (e.g., Reception, Facilities, Lab) | Not eligible for remote work |
| Hybrid-Eligible | Up to 3 days per week remote |
| Fully Remote-Eligible | Fully remote with periodic on-site requirements |
| Manager / Director | Hybrid; must be on-site minimum 2 days per week |

The employee's role classification appears in their employment agreement and is maintained in the HR system. Employees may request a classification review via HR.

---

## 3. Standard Remote Work (Within Primary Work State)

Employees working remotely within their primary state of employment do not require additional approval beyond their manager's standard remote work agreement. The agreement must be documented in BambooHR and updated annually.

---

## 4. Remote Work from a Different US State

Working remotely from a US state other than the employee's primary work state for **more than 14 consecutive calendar days** triggers tax nexus and payroll registration obligations for Acme Corp in that state.

### 4.1 Approval Requirements for Out-of-State Remote Work

Employees must obtain the following approvals **before** beginning out-of-state remote work:

1. **Direct manager approval** — written, via email or BambooHR
2. **HR approval** — HR will verify payroll and benefits implications
3. **Finance/Tax approval** — Required if the duration exceeds 30 days; Finance will assess state tax obligations

The approval request must include:
- Destination state
- Start and end dates of the remote period
- Business justification (if any)
- Confirmation of a stable internet connection and secure workspace

### 4.2 Permitted Duration

Out-of-state remote work is permitted for up to **6 weeks (42 calendar days)** per calendar year per employee. Requests exceeding 6 weeks require VP-level approval and a formal review by Legal and Finance.

---

## 5. International Remote Work

Working remotely from outside the United States is subject to additional legal, tax, and immigration considerations. International remote work is **not automatically approved** and requires:

1. **VP-level or above approval**
2. **Legal review** — Immigration status, work authorization, and host country labor laws
3. **Finance review** — Permanent establishment risk assessment
4. **IT Security review** — Data transfer and access control assessment

International remote work requests must be submitted at least **30 calendar days** in advance. Acme Corp does not guarantee approval of international remote work requests.

---

## 6. Security Requirements for Remote Work

All remote employees must comply with the Data Security Policy (POL-006). Key requirements include:

- **VPN**: All work on Acme Corp systems must be conducted through the company VPN. Employees traveling internationally must use the designated international VPN endpoint.
- **Device security**: Only company-issued or IT-approved devices may be used to access Acme Corp systems. Personal devices require Mobile Device Management (MDM) enrollment.
- **Network security**: Public Wi-Fi (coffee shops, hotels, airports) must not be used without VPN. Home networks must use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption.
- **Physical security**: Screens must not be visible to others. Documents containing confidential information must not be left unattended.
- **Incident reporting**: Any suspected security incident must be reported to IT Security within 1 hour of discovery.

---

## 7. Equipment for Remote Work

Acme Corp will provide standard remote work equipment for approved remote employees, subject to availability. See Equipment Policy (POL-009) for details. Employees may not expense home office furniture under standard remote work arrangements; see the Expense Policy (POL-003) for the home office stipend program.

---

## 8. Performance Expectations

Remote work is a privilege, not a right. Remote employees are expected to:
- Be available and responsive during core business hours (10:00 AM–3:00 PM local time in their manager's time zone)
- Attend all required meetings via video with camera enabled unless otherwise agreed
- Maintain the same performance standards as on-site employees

Managers may rescind remote work arrangements if performance standards are not met, with 5 business days' written notice.

---

## 9. Approval Process Summary

| Scenario | Approvers Required |
|---|---|
| Standard remote (within primary state) | Manager |
| Out-of-state, ≤14 days | Manager + HR notification |
| Out-of-state, 15–42 days | Manager + HR + Finance (if >30 days) |
| Out-of-state, >42 days/year | Manager + HR + Finance + VP |
| International | Manager + HR + Legal + Finance + VP |

---

## 10. Violations

Failure to obtain required approvals before working remotely from another state or country may result in disciplinary action and may expose Acme Corp to tax and legal penalties. Such violations will be escalated to Legal and Finance.
