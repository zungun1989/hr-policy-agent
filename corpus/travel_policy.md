# Business Travel Policy
**Document ID:** POL-010  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** November 2024  
**Owner:** Finance

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs all business travel undertaken by Acme Corp employees. It covers approval requirements, booking guidelines, safety obligations, and reimbursement. Expense reimbursement details are in the Expense Policy (POL-003).

---

## 2. Travel Approval

All business travel must be pre-approved **before** booking. Approval requirements:

| Travel Type | Approver(s) Required |
|---|---|
| Domestic travel (within contiguous US) | Direct manager |
| Travel to Alaska, Hawaii, or US territories | Manager + VP |
| International travel | Manager + VP + Finance |
| Travel during a company-wide travel freeze | CEO or COO only |

Approval must be obtained via the Travel Request form in Concur (Acme Corp's travel and expense platform). Once approved, travel must be booked through Concur or the designated travel agent (TravelBooking portal). Bookings made outside the approved platform may not be reimbursed.

---

## 3. Booking Guidelines

### 3.1 Flights

- Book economy class for all domestic flights.
- Economy class for international flights under 8 hours.
- Business class for international flights 8+ hours requires VP pre-approval.
- Book at least **14 days in advance** when travel is foreseeable.
- Non-refundable tickets are acceptable for confirmed travel; refundable tickets require Finance approval due to higher cost.
- Employees may accumulate personal frequent flyer miles on business travel. Acme Corp does not claim these miles.

### 3.2 Rail

Rail travel (Amtrak, Eurostar, etc.) may be used in lieu of flights for trips under 4 hours. Business class rail requires manager approval. Rail is preferred over flying for short routes when cost and time are comparable, as it reduces carbon footprint.

### 3.3 Hotels

Book hotels through the Concur platform for access to negotiated corporate rates. Nightly room limits (room rate only, excluding taxes):
- **Domestic standard**: $200/night
- **High-cost domestic cities** (NYC, SF, Chicago, DC, Boston, Seattle): $300/night
- **International**: Varies; approved per-destination rates are listed in the Concur travel portal

Employees must not book suites or club-level rooms unless that is the only availability at the city cap rate.

### 3.4 Car Rental

- Rent economy or compact class vehicles.
- Always decline the rental agency's collision damage waiver (CDW) — Acme Corp's corporate travel insurance covers this.
- Refuel the rental vehicle before return to avoid fuel surcharges.
- GPS and car seats for dependents are reimbursable; satellite radio and prepaid toll passes for personal convenience are not.

---

## 4. Travel Safety

### 4.1 Traveler Registration

All employees traveling internationally must register their trip in the Acme Corp Travel Registry (via IT portal) at least **5 business days before departure**. This enables the company to locate and assist employees in case of emergency.

### 4.2 Travel Advisories

Employees must check the US Department of State travel advisory level for international destinations before travel. Travel to Level 3 (Reconsider Travel) or Level 4 (Do Not Travel) countries requires specific VP + Legal approval and a documented risk mitigation plan. Acme Corp reserves the right to prohibit travel to high-risk destinations.

### 4.3 Travel Insurance

Acme Corp provides travel insurance for all approved business travel, covering medical emergencies, trip cancellation, and lost luggage. Policy details are available in the Finance portal. The insurance emergency line is 1-800-555-TRVL.

### 4.4 Security While Traveling

Follow all provisions of the Data Security Policy (POL-006) while traveling:
- Use VPN on all hotel and public Wi-Fi
- Do not leave devices unattended in hotel rooms without locking them in the safe
- Be aware of shoulder surfing in public spaces when working on confidential materials
- Avoid customs-sensitive countries where company data on devices may be subject to inspection — contact IT Security before travel to high-risk regions

---

## 5. Combining Business and Personal Travel

Employees may extend business travel for personal vacation at their own expense. In this case:
- Only the business portions of travel are reimbursable
- Acme Corp will reimburse only what the lowest-cost business-only itinerary would have cost
- Any incremental costs for extending the trip are the employee's responsibility
- Personal extensions must be disclosed to Finance during the travel request

---

## 6. Travel with Dependents

Travel expenses for dependents (spouses, children, guests) are not reimbursable by Acme Corp under any circumstances, even if traveling on the same itinerary.

---

## 7. Travel Cancellations

If approved travel must be cancelled:
- Cancel bookings through Concur or the travel agent as soon as possible to minimize costs
- Non-refundable flight credits remain in the employee's name and must be applied to future Acme Corp business travel
- Document the cancellation reason in Concur for Finance records

---

## 8. Expense Reporting

All travel expenses must be submitted via Concur within **30 days** of return. All receipts for expenses above $75 must be attached. See Expense Policy (POL-003) for detailed expense limits and categories.

Contact travel@acmecorp.com or submit a ticket in Concur Help for travel policy questions.
