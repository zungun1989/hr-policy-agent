# Expense Reimbursement Policy
**Document ID:** POL-003  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** November 2024  
**Owner:** Finance & Human Resources

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs reimbursement of business expenses incurred by Acme Corp employees. Acme Corp reimburses reasonable, necessary, and pre-approved business expenses. All expenses must be submitted within 60 days of being incurred.

---

## 2. General Principles

- Expenses must be **business-related** and **necessary**.
- Employees should exercise the same care spending company funds as they would their own.
- All expenses above $75 require a **receipt**.
- Expenses above $500 require **pre-approval** from the employee's manager before being incurred.
- Expenses above $2,000 require **VP-level pre-approval**.

---

## 3. Technology and Equipment

### 3.1 Laptop and Computer Equipment

Acme Corp provides all employees with a standard-issue laptop upon hire (see Equipment Policy, POL-009). Employees may **not** expense a personal laptop for reimbursement as a substitute for the company-provided device.

Upgrades or accessories (external monitors, keyboards, docking stations) may be expensed up to **$300 per year** with manager approval. Items exceeding this limit require VP approval.

### 3.2 Mobile Phone

Employees who use their personal mobile phone for business may claim a **monthly mobile stipend of $50**. This is a flat stipend, not a reimbursement; no receipts are required. Employees with a company-issued phone are not eligible for the mobile stipend.

---

## 4. Home Office Equipment

### 4.1 Home Office Stipend

Approved remote employees (per Remote Work Policy, POL-002) are eligible for a **one-time home office setup stipend of $750** and an **annual refresh stipend of $200** to maintain a productive home work environment.

**Eligible items**: Desk, ergonomic chair, monitor stand, desk lamp, webcam, headset, and similar productivity equipment.

**Ineligible items**: Decorative items, appliances (coffee makers, refrigerators, etc.), general home furnishings, or items primarily for personal use.

### 4.2 Reimbursement Procedure

Submit receipts for home office expenses via the Concur expense system within 30 days of purchase. Mark the expense category as "Home Office — Remote Stipend." Purchases exceeding the stipend amount will not be reimbursed for the overage.

---

## 5. Business Travel

### 5.1 Pre-Approval

All business travel must be pre-approved by the employee's manager. International travel additionally requires VP approval. Travel arrangements should be booked through Acme Corp's preferred travel provider (TravelBooking portal) to obtain corporate rates.

### 5.2 Airfare

- Book economy class for domestic flights under 6 hours.
- Business class may be approved for flights over 6 hours with VP pre-approval.
- Book at least **14 days in advance** when possible to secure the best rates.
- Upgrades purchased personally are not reimbursable.

### 5.3 Hotels

- Use Acme Corp's preferred hotel partners where available.
- Daily hotel limits (excluding taxes):
  - **Domestic (standard cities)**: $200/night
  - **High-cost cities** (NYC, SF, Boston, DC): $300/night
  - **International**: As approved per destination

### 5.4 Meals and Per Diem

- **Daily meal per diem (domestic)**: $75/day (includes all meals)
- **Daily meal per diem (international)**: $100/day
- Alcohol is **not reimbursable** unless explicitly included as part of a client entertainment event pre-approved by a VP.
- Team meals where the employee is the host: reimbursable with itemized receipt; alcohol limited to 20% of the total.

### 5.5 Ground Transportation

- Rideshare (Uber, Lyft) or taxis to/from airports and business locations: reimbursable with receipt.
- Car rentals: Economy class only; fuel and tolls reimbursable with receipts.
- Mileage reimbursement for personal vehicle use: current IRS rate (2025: $0.67/mile).
- Parking at the office: not reimbursable (subsidized transit benefits available).

---

## 6. Client Entertainment

Client entertainment expenses (dinners, events, gifts) must be pre-approved by a Director or above and include:
- Names and companies of all attendees
- Business purpose
- Itemized receipt

**Limits**: Up to $150/person for meals, $500/event for other entertainment. Gifts to clients are capped at $50/person/year.

---

## 7. Software and Subscriptions

Individual software subscriptions must be pre-approved by the IT department. Software required for a specific project role may be expensed with manager and IT approval. Employees should check the company software catalog before purchasing; many tools are already available via enterprise license.

---

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are explicitly **not reimbursable**:
- Personal travel or personal portions of combined business/personal trips
- Traffic violations, parking tickets, or fines
- Personal grooming (haircuts, clothing, dry cleaning)
- Gym memberships or fitness equipment
- Newspapers, magazines, or books for personal use
- Lost, stolen, or damaged personal items
- Expenses from unauthorized travel

---

## 9. Expense Submission

Submit all expenses via **Concur** within 60 days. Late submissions may not be reimbursed. Attach receipts for all items above $75. Reimbursements are processed within 2 pay cycles of approval.

---

## 10. Violations

Submitting false or inflated expense claims is grounds for immediate termination and may result in legal action. Managers who approve fraudulent expenses share responsibility.

---

Contact finance@acmecorp.com for expense policy questions or submit a ticket in the Finance Help portal.
