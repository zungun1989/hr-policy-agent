# Employee Benefits Policy
**Document ID:** POL-004  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** November 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources — Total Rewards

---

## 1. Overview

Acme Corp offers a comprehensive benefits package to eligible employees. Benefits eligibility, enrollment periods, and plan details are described in this policy. For specific plan documents and Summary Plan Descriptions (SPDs), employees should refer to the Benefits Portal (benefits.acmecorp.com).

---

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees

Full-time employees (working 30 or more hours per week) are eligible for all benefits described in this policy, effective on the **first day of the month following 30 days of employment** (e.g., if hired June 10, benefits begin August 1).

### 2.2 Part-Time Employees

Part-time employees (20–29 hours/week) are eligible for medical insurance only, at a higher employee contribution rate. They are not eligible for dental, vision, 401(k) match, life insurance, or commuter benefits.

### 2.3 Contractors and Temporary Workers

Contractors and temporary employees are not eligible for any benefits under this policy.

---

## 3. Medical Insurance

### 3.1 Plan Options

Acme Corp offers three medical plan options through BlueCross BlueShield:

| Plan | Deductible (Individual) | Out-of-Pocket Max | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| PPO Select | $500 | $3,000 | PPO |
| PPO Plus | $1,000 | $5,000 | PPO |
| HDHP with HSA | $1,500 | $4,000 | High-Deductible |

### 3.2 Premium Contributions (Monthly, Employee Share)

| Plan | Employee Only | Employee + Spouse | Employee + Child(ren) | Family |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PPO Select | $120 | $320 | $280 | $450 |
| PPO Plus | $80 | $220 | $190 | $310 |
| HDHP with HSA | $40 | $150 | $130 | $240 |

Acme Corp covers the remainder of the premium cost.

### 3.3 Health Savings Account (HSA)

Employees enrolled in the HDHP are eligible to open an HSA. Acme Corp contributes:
- **$500/year** for employee-only coverage
- **$1,000/year** for any dependent coverage tier

Employees may contribute pre-tax up to the IRS annual limit ($4,150 individual / $8,300 family in 2025).

---

## 4. Dental Insurance

Dental coverage is provided through Delta Dental. One plan is available:
- **Annual maximum**: $2,000 per covered individual
- **Preventive care** (cleanings, x-rays): 100% covered, no deductible
- **Basic services** (fillings, extractions): 80% covered after $50 deductible
- **Major services** (crowns, bridges): 50% covered after $50 deductible
- **Orthodontia**: 50% covered, lifetime max $1,500 per individual

**Monthly premium**: $18 (employee only), $42 (employee + dependents).

---

## 5. Vision Insurance

Vision coverage is provided through VSP:
- **Eye exam**: Covered once per calendar year (no copay with in-network provider)
- **Frames or contacts**: $150 allowance per year
- **Additional lens options**: 20% discount on upgrades

**Monthly premium**: $8 (employee only), $20 (employee + dependents).

---

## 6. Life Insurance and AD&D

- **Basic life insurance**: 2× annual base salary, provided at no cost to the employee (up to $500,000).
- **Accidental Death & Dismemberment (AD&D)**: Equal to basic life benefit, no cost to employee.
- **Supplemental life**: Employees may purchase up to 5× base salary in additional coverage at group rates.
- **Dependent life**: Spouse coverage up to $50,000; child coverage up to $10,000 per child.

---

## 7. 401(k) Retirement Plan

Acme Corp offers a 401(k) plan through Fidelity.

- **Eligibility**: Full-time employees after 90 days of employment.
- **Employee contribution**: Up to IRS limits ($23,500 in 2025; $31,000 for age 50+).
- **Employer match**: Acme Corp matches 100% of the first 3% of salary deferred, and 50% of the next 2% (up to 4% total employer match).
- **Vesting**: Employer match vests on a 3-year graded schedule: 33% after year 1, 66% after year 2, 100% after year 3.
- **Investment options**: A broad range of index funds, target-date funds, and a self-directed brokerage option.

---

## 8. Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)

Full-time employees may enroll in:
- **Healthcare FSA**: Up to $3,200/year pre-tax (not available if enrolled in HDHP).
- **Dependent Care FSA**: Up to $5,000/year per household.
- **Grace period**: Unused FSA funds may be used up to March 15 of the following year.

---

## 9. Other Benefits

### 9.1 Commuter Benefits

Pre-tax transit and parking benefits up to IRS limits ($315/month for transit; $315/month for parking in 2025).

### 9.2 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

All employees and household members have access to a no-cost EAP offering counseling (up to 6 sessions/year), legal consultation, and financial planning services. Contact: 1-800-555-EAP1.

### 9.3 Wellness Stipend

Full-time employees receive a **$600/year wellness stipend** (paid quarterly at $150) usable for gym memberships, fitness apps, wellness retreats, or nutrition coaching. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days.

### 9.4 Professional Development

Up to $2,500/year for approved professional development expenses (courses, certifications, conferences). Requires manager and HR pre-approval. See the Training Policy for details.

---

## 10. Open Enrollment

Annual open enrollment occurs each November for benefits effective January 1 of the following year. Employees who miss open enrollment cannot change benefits until the next open enrollment unless they experience a qualifying life event (marriage, divorce, birth, adoption, loss of other coverage).

---

## 11. COBRA

Upon separation or loss of eligibility, employees may continue health, dental, and vision coverage under COBRA for up to 18 months at their own expense (employer subsidy ends). HR will provide COBRA election notices within 14 days of the qualifying event.
