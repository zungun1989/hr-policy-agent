# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy
**Document ID:** POL-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** November 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the guidelines for Paid Time Off (PTO) at Acme Corp. PTO provides employees with paid leave for vacation, personal time, and short-term illness in a single, flexible bank of hours.

---

## 2. Eligibility

All full-time employees (working 30 or more hours per week) are eligible for PTO beginning on their first day of employment. Part-time employees working between 20 and 29 hours per week receive PTO at a prorated rate of 50%. Contractors and temporary employees are not eligible for PTO under this policy.

---

## 3. PTO Accrual Rates

PTO accrues on a per-pay-period basis (biweekly). The accrual rate depends on the employee's years of service:

| Years of Service | Annual PTO Days | Per Pay Period (hours) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 15 days (120 hours) | 4.62 hours |
| 3–5 years | 20 days (160 hours) | 6.15 hours |
| 6–10 years | 25 days (200 hours) | 7.69 hours |
| 10+ years | 30 days (240 hours) | 9.23 hours |

New hires begin accruing PTO from their first pay period. However, PTO may not be used until the employee has completed 90 days of employment (the introductory period), except in cases of documented medical necessity approved by HR.

---

## 4. PTO Request and Approval Process

### 4.1 Advance Notice Requirements

Employees must submit PTO requests in the HR system (BambooHR) with the following minimum advance notice:

- **1–2 days PTO**: At least 3 business days in advance
- **3–5 days PTO**: At least 5 business days in advance
- **More than 5 days PTO**: At least 10 business days (2 weeks) in advance
- **Emergency/unplanned PTO**: Notify your direct manager before or within 2 hours of the start of your scheduled shift

### 4.2 Manager Approval

All PTO requests require approval from the employee's direct manager. Managers must respond to PTO requests within 2 business days of submission. Managers should consider:

- Team coverage and business continuity
- Project deadlines and client commitments
- Other team members' approved leave during the same period

Managers may not unreasonably deny PTO requests. If a request is denied, the manager must provide a written reason via the HR system and work with the employee to find an alternative date.

### 4.3 HR Notification

For PTO requests of 5 or more consecutive business days, the manager must also notify HR at least 10 business days in advance. HR retains a right of review for extended leave periods.

---

## 5. PTO Balances and Carryover

### 5.1 Maximum Carryover

Employees may carry over up to 10 days (80 hours) of unused PTO from one calendar year to the next. Any PTO balance above 80 hours as of December 31 will be forfeited.

### 5.2 Payout Upon Separation

Upon voluntary or involuntary separation, accrued but unused PTO up to the carryover maximum (80 hours) will be paid out in the employee's final paycheck, subject to applicable state laws. Employees who are terminated for cause may have their PTO payout waived at the discretion of HR, where permitted by law.

---

## 6. PTO During Notice Period

Employees serving a voluntary resignation notice period may use accrued PTO during their notice period only with explicit written approval from their manager. Acme Corp reserves the right to accept or reject PTO use during a notice period without affecting the accrued balance payout.

---

## 7. Interaction with Other Leave

PTO is separate from the following leave types, which have their own policies:
- **FMLA / Parental Leave** (see Leave Policy, POL-008)
- **Bereavement Leave** (see Leave Policy, POL-008)
- **Jury Duty Leave** (see Leave Policy, POL-008)
- **Holiday Pay** (see Holiday Calendar, POL-011)

Employees must exhaust PTO before taking unpaid leave, except where prohibited by applicable law.

---

## 8. Reporting and Recordkeeping

Employees are responsible for accurately recording all PTO in BambooHR. Falsification of time records is grounds for immediate termination. Managers should audit their team's PTO records quarterly.

---

## 9. Policy Violations

Failure to follow the PTO request process (e.g., taking unapproved PTO, excessive unplanned absences) may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, in accordance with the Workplace Conduct Policy (POL-007).

---

Contact HR at hr@acmecorp.com or submit a ticket via the HR Portal for questions about your PTO balance or this policy.
