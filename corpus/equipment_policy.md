# Equipment and Asset Policy
**Document ID:** POL-009  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** November 2024  
**Owner:** IT & Finance

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how Acme Corp equipment and assets are issued, used, maintained, and returned. All employees are responsible for the proper care of company property in their possession.

---

## 2. Standard Equipment Issued Upon Hire

All full-time employees receive the following equipment on or before their start date:

| Item | Specification | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop | 14" MacBook Pro or Dell XPS (role-dependent) | Configured by IT |
| Power adapter | Matching to laptop | |
| Webcam | Logitech C920 or equivalent | If not built into laptop |
| Headset | Jabra Evolve2 or equivalent | |
| Mouse & keyboard | Wired or wireless standard kit | |
| Monitor (on-site) | 24" 1080p or 27" 4K | For office desk; remote employees see Section 4 |

Part-time employees receive a laptop and power adapter only. Contractors are expected to use their own equipment unless otherwise specified in their contract.

---

## 3. Equipment Care and Acceptable Use

- Employees must keep equipment clean and in good working order.
- Laptops must not be left in vehicles (thermal damage risk) or in publicly accessible areas unattended.
- Physical damage resulting from negligence (e.g., dropping, liquid spills due to carelessness) may result in the employee being charged for repair or replacement costs up to $500 per incident.
- Software installation is governed by the Data Security Policy (POL-006). Only IT-approved software may be installed.
- Equipment may be used for **incidental personal use** (browsing, email) that does not interfere with work performance and does not involve Restricted company data. Excessive personal use is prohibited.

---

## 4. Equipment for Remote Employees

Approved remote employees (per Remote Work Policy, POL-002) receive the standard equipment kit shipped to their home address. In addition:

- Remote employees are eligible for one **external monitor** (up to $400 value) funded by the IT asset budget, approved by their manager.
- Remote employees may also use the **Home Office Stipend** (per Expense Policy, POL-003) for additional peripherals.
- IT support for remote equipment is available via Slack (#it-help) and video call.

---

## 5. Equipment Upgrades and Replacements

### 5.1 Refresh Cycle

Laptops are eligible for replacement after **3 years** of use. IT tracks asset age and proactively initiates refresh cycles. Employees may request an early refresh if the device is significantly affecting productivity; such requests are subject to IT and manager approval.

### 5.2 Damage and Loss

- Report any lost or stolen equipment to IT Security immediately (security@acmecorp.com) and to your manager.
- Lost or stolen company devices must be reported within **4 hours** of discovery to allow remote wipe.
- Theft should also be reported to local law enforcement; provide the police report number to IT.
- Intentional damage or loss due to gross negligence may result in the employee being responsible for replacement costs.

---

## 6. Software Licenses

Software licenses assigned to an employee's device are company property. Employees must not:
- Transfer licenses to personal devices
- Share license keys or credentials with non-employees
- Use company software for personal commercial projects

---

## 7. Return of Equipment

### 7.1 Upon Separation

Employees must return all company equipment within **2 business days** of their last working day. For remote employees, IT will arrange a prepaid shipping label. Failure to return equipment within 30 days will result in the fair market value of the equipment being deducted from the final paycheck, where permitted by law.

### 7.2 What to Return

- Laptop (with original power adapter)
- Webcam and headset
- Any other peripherals issued by IT
- Company badge and access cards (to HR or Facilities)

Employees should not attempt to wipe or reset devices before returning them — IT performs secure data destruction.

---

## 8. Personal Device Use (BYOD)

Employees using personal devices to access Acme Corp systems must comply with the Data Security Policy (POL-006) BYOD requirements. Acme Corp is not responsible for wear and tear on personal devices. MDM profiles installed on personal devices will be removed by IT upon separation.

---

## 9. Questions

Contact IT at it-help@acmecorp.com for equipment requests, repairs, or returns.
