# Employee Onboarding Policy
**Document ID:** POL-005  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** November 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the onboarding process for new employees at Acme Corp to ensure a consistent, welcoming, and effective integration experience.

---

## 2. Pre-Boarding (Before Start Date)

HR will send the following to the new hire at least **5 business days before the start date**:
- Offer letter and employment agreement (if not already signed)
- I-9 Employment Eligibility Verification instructions
- Benefits enrollment invitation
- IT account provisioning request (sent automatically to IT)
- Welcome email with first-day instructions, dress code, parking info, and manager contact

The hiring manager is responsible for:
- Preparing the new hire's workspace (desk, equipment)
- Requesting system access and application permissions via IT ticketing
- Assigning a buddy (peer mentor) from the team

---

## 3. Day 1 Checklist

### 3.1 HR Responsibilities
- Conduct orientation (in-person or virtual for remote hires)
- Review employee handbook and key policies (this document, PTO, Conduct, etc.)
- Complete I-9 verification
- Confirm benefits enrollment or direct to open enrollment if not within first 30 days
- Issue employee badge and parking pass (if on-site)

### 3.2 IT Responsibilities
- Provide laptop and peripherals
- Configure email, Slack, and core application access
- Conduct basic security awareness briefing (password policy, VPN, phishing awareness)

### 3.3 Manager Responsibilities
- Welcome the new hire and introduce the team
- Walk through team processes, tools, and norms
- Schedule recurring 1:1 meetings
- Share the 30/60/90-day plan

---

## 4. First 30 Days

The first 30 days focus on learning:

**Mandatory training (to be completed within 30 days):**
- Data Security Awareness Training (assigned via LMS)
- Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment training
- Privacy and GDPR basics (for roles with data access)
- Role-specific compliance training (varies by department)

**Recommended activities:**
- Meet with key stakeholders and cross-functional partners
- Review product documentation or codebase overview
- Shadow team members on current projects

**HR check-in**: HR will schedule a 15-minute check-in call at day 15 to address any concerns.

---

## 5. Days 31–60

The employee transitions from learning to contributing:
- Begin contributing to real projects with manager guidance
- Complete any outstanding mandatory training
- Participate in team rituals (sprint planning, all-hands, etc.)
- Manager completes a mid-point check-in form in BambooHR at day 45

---

## 6. Days 61–90 and End of Introductory Period

The 90-day introductory period concludes at day 90:
- Manager completes a **90-Day Performance Review** in BambooHR
- The review covers: role understanding, culture fit, performance indicators, and goals for the next quarter
- If the review is satisfactory, the employee transitions to regular status and becomes eligible for PTO use (if not already in use for documented medical need)
- If performance is unsatisfactory, HR and the manager may extend the introductory period by up to 30 additional days or initiate a performance improvement plan

---

## 7. Buddy Program

Each new hire is assigned a buddy (peer mentor) from their team. The buddy's role is to:
- Answer day-to-day questions about team culture and tools
- Be a social touchpoint during the first 90 days
- Not serve as a performance evaluator

Buddies should check in with the new hire at least weekly during the first month and biweekly thereafter. Buddy participation is voluntary but strongly encouraged.

---

## 8. Remote Onboarding

Remote new hires follow the same checklist but with the following adaptations:
- Equipment is shipped to the employee's home address at least 2 business days before start date
- Day 1 orientation is conducted via video call (Zoom)
- IT provides a self-service setup guide; IT support is available via Slack/Zoom
- The buddy plays a more active role in helping the remote new hire feel connected

---

## 9. Rehires and Transfers

Employees returning to Acme Corp after a gap of more than 12 months are treated as new hires and complete the full onboarding process. Internal transfers complete an abbreviated onboarding limited to role-specific training and introductions.
