# Leave of Absence Policy
**Document ID:** POL-008  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** November 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the various types of leave available to Acme Corp employees beyond standard PTO. Leave types covered include Family and Medical Leave (FMLA), Parental Leave, Bereavement Leave, Military Leave, and Jury Duty Leave.

---

## 2. Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)

### 2.1 Eligibility

Employees are eligible for FMLA leave if they have:
- Been employed at Acme Corp for at least **12 months**, AND
- Worked at least **1,250 hours** in the past 12 months, AND
- Work at a location where Acme Corp employs 50 or more employees within 75 miles

### 2.2 Qualifying Reasons

Eligible employees may take up to **12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave** per rolling 12-month period for:
- The birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child
- A serious health condition of the employee
- Care for a spouse, child, or parent with a serious health condition
- Qualifying exigency arising from a family member's military deployment

For care of a seriously ill or injured servicemember, employees may take up to **26 weeks** in a single 12-month period.

### 2.3 Pay During FMLA

FMLA leave is unpaid. Employees must exhaust all accrued PTO concurrently with FMLA leave before taking unpaid days, unless otherwise required by applicable state law.

### 2.4 Notice and Documentation

Employees must provide **30 days' advance notice** when the leave is foreseeable, or as soon as practicable for unforeseen circumstances. HR will provide the required FMLA paperwork (WH-380 or equivalent) within 5 business days of the leave request. Completed certification must be returned within 15 calendar days.

---

## 3. Parental Leave

### 3.1 Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed **6 months of employment** are eligible for paid parental leave upon the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.

### 3.2 Entitlement

| Parent Role | Paid Leave Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Primary caregiver | 12 weeks at 100% base salary |
| Secondary caregiver | 4 weeks at 100% base salary |

Parental leave must be taken within **12 months** of the qualifying event. It may be taken in one continuous block or, with manager approval, in multiple increments of at least one week.

### 3.3 Interaction with FMLA

Paid parental leave runs concurrently with FMLA where FMLA applies. Employees who are not FMLA-eligible still receive parental leave under this policy.

### 3.4 Return-to-Work

Employees returning from parental leave are entitled to return to the same or an equivalent position. A return-to-work meeting with the manager and HR is recommended before the return date to discuss any transition needs.

---

## 4. Bereavement Leave

Employees may take paid bereavement leave upon the death of an immediate family member:

| Relationship | Paid Leave |
|---|---|
| Spouse, domestic partner, child | 5 business days |
| Parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild | 3 business days |
| In-law (parent, sibling) | 2 business days |
| Other close relative or friend (at manager discretion) | 1 business day |

Additional unpaid leave or PTO may be used with manager approval. Documentation (e.g., obituary or death certificate) may be requested. Bereavement leave may be taken non-consecutively within 30 days of the death with manager approval.

---

## 5. Military Leave

Employees who are called to active military duty or training are entitled to leave in accordance with the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA). Key provisions:
- Job protection for leave up to 5 cumulative years
- Reinstatement rights upon return
- Continuation of health benefits during leave (at employee's expense beyond 30 days)
- Military leave is unpaid beyond the first 2 weeks, which Acme Corp supplements to base salary

Employees must provide advance notice of military leave when possible. Contact HR to arrange leave.

---

## 6. Jury Duty Leave

Employees summoned for jury duty or subpoenaed to testify in court are entitled to leave. Acme Corp pays the difference between jury duty pay and the employee's regular base salary for up to 10 business days. Beyond 10 days, the employee may use PTO or take unpaid leave.

Employees must provide their jury summons to HR and their manager as soon as received. If released from jury duty early in a given day, employees are expected to return to work for the remainder of the business day if reasonably practicable.

---

## 7. Requesting Leave

To request any type of leave covered by this policy:
1. Notify your manager as early as possible
2. Submit a leave request via the HR Portal (or email hr@acmecorp.com if the portal is unavailable)
3. Complete any required documentation provided by HR
4. Receive written confirmation from HR before the leave begins (except in emergencies)

---

## 8. Questions

Contact HR at hr@acmecorp.com for questions about any leave type, eligibility, or documentation requirements.
