# Workplace Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy
**Document ID:** POL-007  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** November 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources & Legal

---

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corp is committed to maintaining a workplace free from discrimination, harassment, and retaliation. This policy defines expected standards of professional conduct and the process for reporting and resolving workplace concerns.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, temporary workers, interns, and vendors when present at Acme Corp premises, attending company events, or representing Acme Corp in any capacity. It applies to conduct in person, via email, phone, messaging platforms (Slack, Teams), and social media when related to work or colleagues.

---

## 3. Expected Standards of Conduct

All individuals covered by this policy must:
- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect and professionalism
- Communicate honestly and transparently
- Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and company policies
- Protect company assets, intellectual property, and confidential information
- Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose any that arise
- Maintain appropriate professional boundaries
- Not engage in behavior that creates a hostile, intimidating, or offensive work environment

---

## 4. Prohibited Conduct

### 4.1 Harassment

Harassment is any unwanted conduct that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive environment, or that unreasonably interferes with an individual's work performance. Harassment may be verbal, visual, physical, or electronic and includes:

- Offensive jokes, slurs, or derogatory comments based on any protected characteristic
- Displaying offensive images, cartoons, or memes
- Unwelcome physical contact
- Repeated unwelcome invitations of a personal or romantic nature
- Threatening language or gestures
- Cyberbullying or online harassment via company or personal channels in a work context

### 4.2 Sexual Harassment

Sexual harassment includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature when:
- Submission is made a condition of employment (quid pro quo); or
- Such conduct creates a hostile work environment

### 4.3 Discrimination

Discrimination based on any protected characteristic is prohibited. Protected characteristics include: race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age (40+), disability, genetic information, sexual orientation, gender identity, marital status, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law.

### 4.4 Retaliation

Retaliation against any person who, in good faith, reports a concern, participates in an investigation, or exercises their rights under this policy is strictly prohibited. Retaliation is itself a serious violation and may result in termination.

### 4.5 Workplace Violence

Threats or acts of physical violence, including intimidation, coercion, or any behavior that endangers the safety of others, are grounds for immediate termination and referral to law enforcement.

## 5. Reporting Procedures

### 5.1 Who to Report To

Employees experiencing or witnessing prohibited conduct should report to:
1. **Direct manager** (unless the manager is involved in the conduct)
2. **HR Business Partner** — hr@acmecorp.com
3. **Anonymous Ethics Hotline** — 1-800-555-ETHX (24/7, third-party operated)
4. **Legal / Compliance** — legal@acmecorp.com (for matters involving potential legal liability)

### 5.2 How to Report

Reports may be made verbally or in writing. Written reports submitted via the HR Portal or Ethics Hotline are preserved and treated as formal complaints. Employees are encouraged (but not required) to document incidents including: date, time, location, description of conduct, names of witnesses, and any evidence (emails, screenshots).

### 5.3 What Happens After You Report

1. **Acknowledgment**: HR will acknowledge receipt within **1 business day**.
2. **Preliminary review**: HR will assess whether the report warrants a formal investigation (typically within 3 business days).
3. **Investigation**: If warranted, HR and/or Legal will conduct an investigation, which is typically completed within **30 calendar days**.
4. **Outcome**: The reporting employee will be informed that the matter has been addressed, although specific disciplinary actions involving another employee are typically not disclosed due to confidentiality.
5. **Follow-up**: HR will follow up with the reporting employee 30 days after case closure.

---

## 6. HR Case Triage Guidelines

HR triages workplace issues into three tiers:

| Tier | Description | Response |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Policy clarification, minor interpersonal friction | HR coaching and mediation |
| **Tier 2** | Potential policy violation, harassment concern, conduct issues | Formal HR investigation |
| **Tier 3** | Serious legal risk (discrimination claim, violence, criminal conduct) | Legal escalation, external counsel involvement |

HR assigns a triage level within 3 business days of receiving a formal report.

---

## 7. Confidentiality

HR will handle all reports with as much confidentiality as possible, consistent with its obligation to conduct a thorough investigation. Complete anonymity cannot be guaranteed but every effort is made to limit disclosure of the reporter's identity.

---

## 8. Disciplinary Actions

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Verbal or written warning
- Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
- Mandatory training
- Reassignment or transfer
- Suspension (paid or unpaid)
- Termination of employment

Severity of discipline depends on the nature and severity of the violation, prior conduct history, and mitigating or aggravating circumstances. HR makes disciplinary decisions in consultation with Legal and the relevant manager or VP.

---

## 9. Manager Responsibilities

Managers who witness or become aware of potential policy violations must:
- Promptly report to HR — failure to do so may result in disciplinary action for the manager
- Not conduct informal investigations or advise parties involved
- Not retaliate against employees who report concerns
